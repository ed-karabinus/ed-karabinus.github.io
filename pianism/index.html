Pianism Support

Pianism is a piano practice tracker for iPhone and iPad. Time a session with a tap, or connect a digital piano and let it start the timer.

Questions, problems or ideas: email pianism.support@gmail.com.

Your piano

Sessions don't start when I play

Sessions start, pause and save themselves only while Pianism is open on screen. iOS doesn't let any app listen to a piano in the background.

Check that Practice shows your piano's name. If it doesn't, connect it in Settings: over USB with a cable or adapter, or over Bluetooth MIDI. A piano connects to one device at a time, so disconnect it from any other first.

A session started by tapping Start stays a tapped session: playing keeps it going but doesn't pause or end it.

Do I need a piano?

No. Everything else works without one: the timer, tags, the weekly goal, History, the widget and iCloud sync.

Pianism Pro

What's in Pro?

One purchase, no subscription. Pro lets your piano start, pause and save sessions after the first 5 free ones, and adds time per piece and CSV export. To buy it, open Settings and tap Pianism Pro.

I bought Pro, but it's locked on another device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Pianism and tap Restore Purchases. Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud

My sessions don't show on my other device

Pianism syncs through your own iCloud. On each device, sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and turn iCloud on for Pianism in the Settings app. New sessions can take a minute to appear.

There's no Pianism account, and I can't see or restore your sessions: they're stored in your iCloud, not on my servers.
