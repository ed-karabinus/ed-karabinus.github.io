Pianism Privacy Policy

Pianism has no account and no ads. Your practice stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The only thing that leaves the app is a small set of anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Your practice

Your sessions, pieces, skills, tags, notes and goals are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud database, under Apple's privacy policy. I can't see them, and nothing in Pianism sends them anywhere else.

When a digital piano is connected, Pianism listens for notes only to tell when you're playing. It doesn't record or keep what you play: a session keeps only when it started, paused and ended.

Reminders and the "Still practicing?" notification are scheduled on your device.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn whether Pianism is useful, the App Store and TestFlight versions send a few anonymous signals through TelemetryDeck, an analytics service that doesn't store IP addresses. The signals are:

  • that the app opened,
  • that it was opened for the first time,
  • that a session was saved, and whether it was timed with a tap, started by a piano or added by hand,
  • that someone saved 3 sessions in their first week,
  • and that the Pianism Pro screen opened.

TelemetryDeck adds details to each signal: a scrambled ID for this install of the app, so installs can be counted without saying who anyone is; your device model and iOS version; the app's version; your language, region and time zone; display and accessibility settings such as text size; the rough time of day; and how many days you've used the app.

They never include piece or skill names, notes, session times or lengths, your piano's name, anything you typed, or your name, email or Apple Account. Nothing is linked to you, and nothing is used to track you across apps or websites.

To turn it off, open Settings in Pianism and switch off "Share anonymous usage". Nothing further is sent.

Purchases

Pianism Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and I never see your payment details or who you are. Pianism only learns from Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass along anonymous crash reports and usage figures. That's Apple's setting, covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Pianism doesn't ask for anyone's age, name or contact details. The anonymous usage counts above are the same for everyone, and are sent only while "Share anonymous usage" is on.
